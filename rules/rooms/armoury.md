# Armoury

Family: martial. Feel: **orderly, the gear on show**. Kit kind: `gear_store` (a garrison's armoury, a bunkhouse's gear
room). Profile: `kit/roomtypes.py TYPES["armoury"]`.

## Purpose and feel

A crew's arms and armour in good order, ready to hand. Split from the storeroom: a store keeps supplies, an armoury
shows arms on racks (Westwood: 29 rooms of racks with no counter or keeper, more than its storerooms).

## Focal point

The racks, in rows down the middle, each row of one kind.

## Pieces

- Must: racks (3+: armour stands, pole arms, bows and quivers, clothes racks).
- May: shelves end to end on a back wall, helm shelves and hanging swords, barrels, crates and tool barrels by the front
  walls, a stack.
- Never: beds, desks, tables, an altar, a throne, tombs, lab pieces, bars, counters, stoves, hearths, bookcases,
  black-powder barrels.

## Composition

- **A back wall:** shelves end to end.
- **The other back wall:** helm shelves or hanging swords.
- **Front walls:** barrels, crates, tool barrels.
- **The middle:** racks in rows of one kind each, at most five to a row, 1.2 apart, aisles of 2.2 between rows.

## Culture variants

Dun Mir armouries (20 of Westwood's 29) stand among the castle's guard rooms, with tables and chairs for the guards.

## Common mistakes

- "Storeroom racks a little too dense and numerous: spread out, fewer" (2026-10-04 review). The old storeroom
  coverage target (0.30) packed the gear rooms to 0.16-0.37 open floor; the armoury's own is 0.24.
- Racks of mixed kinds in one row.
