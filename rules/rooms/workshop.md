# Workshop

Family: work. Feel: **balanced, busy**. Kit kind: `workshop` (a wheelwright's, a carpenter's, a millwright's shop).
Profile: `kit/roomtypes.py TYPES["workshop"]`.

## Purpose and feel

A craftsman's bench room: the work bench in the middle with the job on it, his tools on shelves along a wall, wheels and
gears waiting by the bench, timber and iron in crates and barrels of tools toward the corners. No forge (that is the
smithy's) and no counter (he sells from the door): the room is for making.

## Focal point

The work bench (a long table) with its stool and a crate or barrel at its end.

## Pieces

- Must: the work bench, tool shelves (TraderShelves), barrels of tools and crates (3+).
- May: a second bench in a big shop, an anvil for the ironwork, a gear train on a wall, cart wheels and small gears
  lying by the bench.
- Never: a forge or bellows, a counter, a bar, beds, desks, bookcases, hearths, stoves, lab pieces, an altar, black
  powder.

## Composition

- **A back wall:** trader's shelves of tools end to end.
- **The other back wall:** a gear train, an anvil toward its corner, or a second run of shelves.
- **Front walls:** tool barrels, crates of timber and iron, heaped toward the corners.
- **The middle:** the bench with its stool, a crate at its end; wheels and parts by it; a clear way round it.

## Size

25-110 tiles (Westwood's: 29).

## Culture variants

A mine's (Ix): steel barrels and crates.

## Where people stand

The craftsman at his bench (at its side, not at its end where the crate is), else by his tool shelves. (`STANDS
["workshop"]`: beside the bench, beside the tool shelves, a back wall.)

## Common mistakes

- A storeroom with a table: the bench must carry the job (parts by it, a stool at it) and the walls the tools.
- Forge pieces: a workshop with a forge is a smithy.

## Examples

- Westwood: Con06a / War01A, cell 102,135 (25-29 tiles: an anvil, a tool barrel, a gear, four small gears, three cart
  wheels, trader's shelves).
- Ours: the room lab's workshops.
