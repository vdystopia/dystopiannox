# Guardroom: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described from the rooms themselves.

## What it is for

Where a watch waits by the door it guards. Westwood's eleven include a gaol's guard room, watch huts, keep guard rooms and dungeon guard posts. Most are small: about 12 to 25 tiles, with two big ones of 55 and 63.

## What you notice first

A table with chairs round it. In about three quarters of them it is a round table, and in two it has a meal on it. One hut has no table, only two cots, chests and straw.

## What it holds

- Common: the table and two to four chairs, often mismatched (dark, old and plain chairs together). Barrels stand by the walls in most. About half have a chest, usually a Dun Mir chest.
- Sometimes: a cot or two (in about a third), arms hung on a wall (swords in three, shields in two), a bench, a second smaller table, a spittoon, a bear's head trophy, straw, a rug under the table.
- Rare: racks of weapons. Only the gaol's guard room has a pole-arm rack, a bow rack, a quiver rack and a hung crossbow.
- Not seen: bars, counters, altars, hearths, bookcases, plants.

## How it is arranged

In the small posts nearly everything is pushed to the walls: barrels in a corner or two, the chest against a wall, cots along a wall, and chairs sometimes backed against a wall instead of drawn up to the table. The table sits in the middle or off to one side. Several have two or three doorways, with the way across left open.

## Density and feel

Lived in and a little untidy: about half the floor open, few pieces crowding a small room.

## Variants

Dun Mir guard rooms have Dun Mir chests and shields hung on the wall. Town and dungeon posts have plain chests, barrels and dark wooden chairs.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

- A chair knocked over on the floor (in two rooms).
- Odd chairs that do not match, and a small table beside a round one.
- Rooms that are rarely symmetric: barrels bunched in one corner, a single arms piece on one wall, the cots both on one side.
