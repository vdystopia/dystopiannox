# Solar: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described by their qualities, not room by room.

## What it is for

A lord's own chamber, larger than a bedroom: one end for sleeping, the other for sitting and receiving.

## What you notice first

The size, and the two ends. The sitting end is anchored by a hearth between bookcases with tables and chairs before it, or by a long table with benches; the bed lies at the other end, sometimes in an alcove of its own. Some carry ceremonial pieces standing in the room as well.

## What it holds

- A bed (now and then two, or a humble cot), sometimes a nightstand.
- Bookcases, often in runs; chests; tables with chairs; benches.
- A rug or bearskin before the bed or the hearth, most often.
- Tapestries, sometimes many of one colour; candelabras.
- Sometimes: a desk, columns, statues, tall stones.
- Not in Westwood's: plants, or the working pieces of a kitchen or laboratory.

## How it is arranged

The room's shape does the zoning: an L-shape, an alcove, a partition across the middle. Bookcases line a wall in runs; the rest stands in groups, and in the bigger chambers much of it stands free of the walls. Open floor separates the two ends.

## Density and feel

Rich but roomy: a dozen to twenty kinds of thing over a large floor, with about two thirds of it open. Some are carpeted wall to wall.

## Variants

Each culture brings its own pieces: hearth, bookcases and tapestries in some; columns, benches at long tables and the culture's chests in others.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

Lived-in oddities: water barrels by the wall, a crate, an open chest, a fallen chair, a spittoon, bellows at the hearth. Colours repeat (a room's tapestries all of one colour).

## Kin rooms

Bedrooms are small: the bed in a corner with a nightstand, a bookcase or two, a chest, often a desk and chair. Living rooms centre on a hearth or stove, a round table with chairs on a rug, bookcases. Studies put a desk among bookcases, sometimes with a hearth or stove.
