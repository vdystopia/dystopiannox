# Laboratory: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described by their qualities, not room by room.

## What it is for

A wizard's workroom for experiments, brewing and reading. Westwood's are town rooms, ranging from a closet to a great hall.

## What you notice first

The wizard's workstations, the tall benches of apparatus. Every lab has them, from one to several, and they are the focal piece. They stand against the walls, often the front walls (the lower walls nearest the viewer, seen from behind), and some sit in corners.

## What it holds

- Common: workstations (every one); a desk with a chair (nearly every one).
- In the bigger rooms: bookcases, a few or many; a round table laid with food and its chairs; a long work table.
- Now and then: other apparatus (a set of small coils), an alchemist's desk, a pair of small statues, a vase, tapestries, a painting, a rug, a fairy jar, shelves of jars, a barrel and crate.
- Absent: cauldrons, plants, candle circles and work islands in the middle.

## How it is arranged

The room is worked along its walls and the middle is left as open floor. Workstations take one or two walls and the desk another, often a back wall (one of the two upper walls in the picture). In the large rooms, bookcases run along the walls, front walls included. Apparatus of a kind stands together along one wall. A table with food sits off to one side.

## Density and feel

Fairly open, with about three fifths of the floor clear. The smallest is a few workstations in a closet. The great ones fill their walls with apparatus and books but keep the floor free. They are lit more brightly than most rooms.

## Variants

All are town rooms. A room with an alchemist's desk and a bed is a bedroom, and a room of glass cases is a gallery.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

The apparatus is of mixed kinds, unevenly grouped and sometimes on the front walls. A meal is left on a table in a workroom, and a barrel and crate sit in a small one. Westwood's labs differ a great deal from one another, with no set layout.
