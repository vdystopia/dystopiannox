# Living room: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described from the rooms themselves.

## What it is for

A household's hearth room, where the family eats and sits by the fire. Westwood has 14, ten in town and four in Dun Mir. Four of them are one-room cottages or huts that have the bed in them too.

## What you notice first

The fire and a table ringed with chairs. Ten rooms have a hearth on a back wall (one of the two upper walls in the picture), with bellows beside it in seven. The others, and one of the ten, have an iron stove, often in a corner.

## What it holds

- In every room: a table with chairs, usually round and often laid with food, with three to eight dark wooden chairs. About half have a second table, square or oval.
- Often: bookcases (half, often beside the hearth); a bench or cushioned bench (about half); barrels or water barrels (about half); a chest (about a third).
- Sometimes: a spittoon (four rooms); a bed or cot in the cottages; a red rug, a tapestry, a painting, a vase; statues and gargoyles in a grand common room. One room has a fallen chair.
- Absent: plants and hunting trophies.

## How it is arranged

Typically three walls are used. The hearth sits on a back wall with chairs drawn up near it, and the way to the fire stays open. The table group stands near the middle but rarely dead centre, often on a carpet laid in the floor. Bookcases, chests and benches stand along the walls, front walls included. Barrels sit in pairs or a small heap.

## Density and feel

Warm, cosy and lived-in. In small rooms a table of food and its chairs fill nearly all the floor. Larger rooms leave half to three quarters of the floor open.

## Variants

Dun Mir rooms have Dun Mir chests, benches and long tables. Cottages fit a bed, a stove, chests and a table into one room.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

The chairs are unevenly spaced, some angled toward the fire, and one may be knocked over. Barrels sit in a corner, a spittoon stands by a chair, and the hearth is on whichever back wall suits the room.
