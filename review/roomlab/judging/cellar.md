# Cellar: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described from the rooms themselves.

## What it is for

A keg cellar: the ale, wine and water of a house or keep, kept in barrels. Westwood has four, so the real rooms on the sheet may also be of the kin store types (a storeroom, a powder store or a treasury).

## What you notice first

Kegs. In two cellars five kegs stand in two corners and nothing else; in another eight kegs crowd together in one tight block. The largest has a great cask standing out from the walls with kegs about it.

## What it holds

- Kegs in all four, five to eight of them.
- Dark crates in two, a couple to four, by the door or beside the kegs.
- Seen once: a great cask, a stack of piled barrels; a floor lever in one.
- Not in Westwood's cellars: shelves, sacks, apple crates, tables, chairs, racks or statues; they have no light inside, or one at most.

## How it is arranged

The kegs gather in knots against the walls, mostly in the corners, sometimes two or three in a short run, with some standing a step out into the room. The floor between the knots is left open; a small cellar may be half bare. The way in from the door is clear.

## Density and feel

Small and plain: 17 to 36 tiles, one to five kinds of thing. A cellar with few kegs is mostly open floor; a packed one leaves only about a third of its floor open.

## Variants

All four are plain store rooms; one lies under Dun Mir's keep and its twin under a human keep, both with kegs only.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

Unevenness: the kegs in two knots of different sizes rather than one even row, a crate or two pushed against the kegs, the great cask standing out on its own. Two keeps share one cellar layout, so a plain, near-empty cellar is as real as a full one.

## Kin rooms

Westwood's storerooms mix their stock: kegs, crates, apple crates, water barrels, shelves of provisions, a chest, sometimes a table and chair or a fallen chair; a mine's store has steel crates, tool barrels and shovels. Its one powder store holds eleven black-powder kegs in rows with two plain kegs, under cobwebs.
