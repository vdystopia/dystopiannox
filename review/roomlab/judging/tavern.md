# Tavern: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described by their qualities, not room by room.

Westwood's tavern rooms are mostly large common rooms; a small inn may split into a barroom and a seating room. Dining halls and shops are its kin.

## What it is for

The public room of an inn: drink at the bar, eat at the tables, warm by the fire.

## What you notice first

The bar: built up of many pieces, with corner pieces and a hinged flap, running along a wall or across a corner. Large casks and piled barrels stand behind or beside it. In a small barroom the bar and its casks fill most of the room.

## What it holds

- Common: many seats of mixed kinds (stools, wooden or dark chairs, cushioned stools, cushioned and light benches), twenty or more in a common room; round tables, round tables with food, long tables; barrels, large barrels, piled barrels, water barrels; spittoons; a fire (a wall fireplace, a freestanding fireplace or a stove).
- Sometimes: a bearskin rug, bookcases and bellows by the fire, small mirrors, paintings, a tapestry, a chest.
- Not seen: plants, statues, beds, shop goods, hunting trophies.

## How it is arranged

The bar keeps to one side with its kegs. Tables and seats spread over the rest: some out in the middle, many pushed against the walls with their chairs. Barrels collect by the bar and in corners. A freestanding fire may stand out on the floor among the tables.

## Density and feel

Lively and full, yet about half the floor stays open between the sets. Common rooms are the most varied rooms in the campaign: a dozen or more kinds of furniture, besides a bar built of many different pieces.

## Variants

Each culture brings its own seats, tables and fire: dark or wooden chairs and stools in some, cushioned stools and benches in others; a wall fireplace, a stove or freestanding fires.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

Chairs knocked over; spittoons; a rug before the fire; bellows and books by the hearth. Sets are uneven, stools and chairs mixed round the same tables. The bar is a long irregular build of many pieces, not a single block.
