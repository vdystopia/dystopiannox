# Tavern: what a real one is like

For the blind judge: Westwood's campaign rooms of this type, described from the rooms themselves.

Westwood's campaign has five tavern rooms: three common rooms (a village's, Dun Mir's and Galava's, 125 to 216 tiles), and one small inn split into a barroom and a seating room. Dining halls and shops are its kin.

## What it is for

The public room of an inn: drink at the bar, eat at the tables, warm by the fire.

## What you notice first

The bar: built up of many pieces, with corner pieces and a hinged flap, running along a wall or across a corner. Large casks and piled barrels stand behind or beside it. In the small barroom the bar and its casks fill most of the room.

## What it holds

- Common: many seats of mixed kinds (stools, wooden or dark chairs, cushioned stools, cushioned and light benches), twenty or more in a common room; round tables, round tables with food, long tables; barrels, large barrels, piled barrels, water barrels; spittoons; a hearth (a wall fireplace, two freestanding fireplaces in Dun Mir, a stove in Galava's).
- Sometimes: a bearskin rug (two of three common rooms), bookcases and bellows by the fire, small mirrors, paintings, a red tapestry, a chest.
- Not seen: plants, statues, beds, shop goods, hunting trophies.

## How it is arranged

The bar keeps to one side with its kegs. Tables and seats spread over the rest: some out in the middle, many pushed against the walls with their chairs. Barrels collect by the bar and in corners. In Dun Mir the two freestanding fireplaces stand out on the floor among the tables.

## Density and feel

Lively and full, yet about half the floor stays open between the sets. Common rooms are the most varied rooms in the campaign: a dozen or more kinds of furniture, besides a bar built of many different pieces.

## Variants

- Town: dark or wooden chairs and stools, round tables with food, a wall fireplace or a stove, mirrors and paintings.
- Dun Mir: cushioned stools and benches round round tables, freestanding fireplaces, water barrels.

## What a designer's hand looks like here

Chairs knocked over; spittoons; a bearskin before the fire; bellows and bookcases in a village inn. Sets are uneven, stools and chairs mixed round the same tables. The bar is a long irregular build of many pieces, not a single block.
