# QA/QC sweep (2026-10-09)

Asked for after the 20 gauntlets: "take what you've learned throughout the entire project so far and do a QA, QC
sweep". Everything installed and every tool was run again, with the current kit and the user's current rules.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|

| Creature placement crash (a lead from the ModLab agent) | **root cause found and fixed** (below) |

## Defects found and fixed

1. **Maps failing to load about one time in eight (the map writer).** OpenNox reads the next section name in whole
   crypt blocks; a map whose object data ended in its last block failed with "cannot read next section: EOF" and a
   panic. Whether a map failed depended only on its byte size: a Skeleton map crashed with a 10-character script name
   and loaded with 9 or 11. The long-standing "never place a Zombie" rule and ModLab's "Skeleton and Wolf cannot be
   placed" were this bug, not the creatures. Fix: `Shared/Map.cs` writes a spare block of zeros; new checker rule
   `setup.file_tail` flags a generated map built without it; `UNPLACEABLE`/`UNSAFE_PLACED` emptied (Zombie,
   VileZombie, Skeleton, Wolf maps load).
2. **The harpoon staff pushed creatures away.** `PushTo` pushes away from the point for a positive force
   (`server/object.go` Push); the reel now uses a negative force. Harpoon map rebuilt.
3. **Scripts overwrote each other's creature events.** An object keeps one callback per event, so a quest's OnDeath,
   a sentry's rouse and a new monster's powers on the same creature cancelled each other. New
   `kit/behaviours/events.go` (OnObjEvent) fans one engine callback out to every subscriber; quests, behaviours and
   mods all use it. Proved in the server (a Bone Caller that is also a quest target).
4. **Mirefen's main quest could not be finished after a rebuild.** Its chapel (and the necromancer in it) was no
   longer generated: a minimum-size rule added later was out of reach at the design's scale. `Dresser.structure`
   now tries larger sizes and says when nothing fits; `StoryMap.keepers` never drops a creature silently.
5. **Tools:** `server_smoke.py` failed on maps without scripts and counted another OpenNox's profiler port as a
   failure; `checkcheck.py` could not write its JSON. Fixed.

## Open items (not fixed in this sweep)

- **Checker calibration** (`validate/out/checkcheck.md`): 9 rules never fire and have no planted case
  (`setup.type_flags`, `minimap.cover`, `doors.on_wall`, `doors.no_half`, `kits.back`, `story.gate`, `rooms.bare`,
  `composition.bridge_bend`, `routes.more`); error rules that fire on many Westwood maps: `wall_shapes.gap` (14),
  `doors.no_wall` (16), `transport.landing` (8), `boundary.hole` (6), and `floors.wall_blend` (107: intended, it is the
  user's own rule TW-12, stricter than Westwood); warnings on over a quarter of Westwood's maps: `composition.way_in`,
  `grammar_lone`, `grammar_corners`. Each needs a look, not a blanket change.
- **TownLab** (41 errors) and **ModLab** (the vault) could accept their known errors in the design (`QA_ACCEPT`).
- **Rivers without shore walls** (found by the act-1 builder): `Waterworks.finish` skips a water tile when the design's
  `inside` predicate is floor-based (`startswith(("Grass", "Dirt"))`, Thornwick's and Brackwater's), and a water tile
  is never grass, so those rivers get almost no invisible shore walls (22 for 804 water tiles): the player can probably
  wade in. The fix (walls on every tile a body dug) adds many walls, which `tests/sightrows.py` counts toward the
  sight-row crash risk, so it goes in after the campaign is built, with both measured together.
- **A planted self-test case for `setup.file_tail`** (a map written without the spare block).
- **Everything that needs a person playing:** the ModLab powers on a real swing and throw, the forge plate, the
  Spirit class's character screen and a fight with it, the gauntlets' balance. A headless e2e input mode exists in
  OpenNox (`NOX_E2E`, found while testing campaign tokens): it could drive a scripted player for these.
- Round-1 ratings: none saved on the rating page yet.

## Knowledge gained (in the kit and the notes)

- Item tokens survive map changes and saves in a real solo game; named items keep their names
  (`review/out/qa/tokens/`); the engine's quest-item list cannot be sold or dropped; keys are eaten by matching doors.
- Creating several items and picking them all up in one frame hangs the game.
- OpenNox has a scripted-input end-to-end mode (`NOX_E2E=<yaml>`) usable for headless play tests.
