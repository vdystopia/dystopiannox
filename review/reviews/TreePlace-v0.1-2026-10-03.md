# TreePlace v0.1 — visual review (2026-10-03)

A new map built from scratch with the refined process (PROCESS.md), after the DysVale v0.6 playtest. Theme: a
sacred mana forest. A giant mana-crystal cluster sits in the heart of a grove. Three themed woods stretch out
to the north, west and south. A long winding path runs east to a mana mine's camp, whose mine entrance is
blocked by a cave-in.

Size: 16,168 floor tiles, about 2.0× DysVale (8,075). The land spans the whole 256 × 256 map grid.

Checker: 0 errors. Two map-wide warnings remain:
- no creatures yet (planned for later);
- 16.9 wall pieces per 100 floor tiles. Westwood's forest maps carry 21 to 52. Thickets raised this from 15.5.

Compared with Con08b, Wiz03b and Wiz01A, the design measurements are within Westwood's forest range. Two are
above it, meaning more structured:
- paths joined into one network: 0.82;
- small plants in single-type clumps: 0.525.
