# QA round 1: mod features (weapons, monsters, the Spirit class)

The user's notes on ModLab in `review/reviews/QA-round1.md` (section Mods), and what answers each. Columns as
`review/FEEDBACK.md`:
- **Rule**: where the behaviour lives. "mods.go" is `mapgen/kit/behaviours/mods.go`; "catalogue" is `mapgen/kit/mods.py` WEAPONS/MONSTERS; "MODLAB" is `mods/MODLAB.md`.
- **Enforced by**:
  - `selftest:<step>` is the dedicated server's self-test line (`py tests/modlab_e2e/lab.py server`, or `py tests/server_smoke.py mapgen/designs/modlab.py --seconds 50`).
  - `play:<probe>` is the real-player run in OpenNox's e2e mode (`py tests/modlab_e2e/lab.py play <probe> <class>`).
  - `look:` is what to judge by eye in a play session.
- **Status**: **check** (an automatic run shows it working) or **review** (only a person can judge it). Before is the kit at b005f84.
