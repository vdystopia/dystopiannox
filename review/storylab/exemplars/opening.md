# The main quest opened (captains, mentors, masters)

Westwood's own lines (Nox, 1999), from the campaign's string table. Imitate their rhythm, register, corniness and how people are named; never copy a line or reuse a Westwood name. Orders, not requests: where to go, whom to find, what to bring back, and the chapter's villain named. Often 'lad'. Grand words beside plain ones.

A page break inside a speech shows as ` / `. The key says who speaks and where (Con = Conjurer, War = Warrior, Wiz = Wizard campaign; the number is the chapter).
