# Shopkeepers, barkeepers, pedlars

Westwood's own lines (Nox, 1999), from the campaign's string table. Imitate their rhythm, register, corniness and how people are named; never copy a line or reuse a Westwood name. A flat slogan, a pitch, a wink at the price or the times, a grumble. A town's keepers have nothing to do with each other.

A page break inside a speech shows as ` / `. The key says who speaks and where (Con = Conjurer, War = Warrior, Wiz = Wizard campaign; the number is the chapter).
