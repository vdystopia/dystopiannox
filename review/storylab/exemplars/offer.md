# Quest offers (a giver's first words)

Westwood's own lines (Nox, 1999), from the campaign's string table. Imitate their rhythm, register, corniness and how people are named; never copy a line or reuse a Westwood name. Most start in the middle of the trouble or with a stock greeting, not with a self-introduction. Some are a page, some three. Not all end on a question; not all say why the giver cannot go.

A page break inside a speech shows as ` / `. The key says who speaks and where (Con = Conjurer, War = Warrior, Wiz = Wizard campaign; the number is the chapter).

## The trouble shouted first

- It's a disaster! Spiders are Mayor Theogrin's greatest fear and a swarm have nested in his study! He's locked himself away and won't come out till the spiders are gone! / A Conjurer named Aldwyn lives outside of town, to the East. We need him to charm the spiders and liberate our besieged Mayor.  `Con02a:MayorsGuard`
- It is the worst of horrors! The Ogres have taken over the Tower!  `Con07B.scr:WardenTalk01`
- Finally! A Conjurer has come to my aid!  `Con02a:MayorCall`
- Help! Help me please!  `War05A.scr:IngridScream`
- Conjurer! Please help us!  `Con07C.scr:OpeningHelp`

## A victim begs

- Please, free me! If the Ogres don't torture me or use me as food, I'll soon be dead anyway. My companion died two days ago and the stench of his rotting corpse is a constant and nauseating reminder of my certain fate.  `War09a:AidanGreet1`
- We were trekking through the swamp when an Ogre patrol spotted us. Horvath sent us up here to spy on Hecubah and observe the movements of her allies. / Please, if you help me by finding the key that opens this cell, I can possibly help you in return.  `War09a:AidanGreet2`
- Please... I can't fend them off much longer!  `War09c:CainPlea`
- Do you have a healing potion to cure a dying man?  `War09c:CainAsk`
- My brother in arms, many thanks for coming to my aid... But too late, I'm afraid. My wounds are mortal. I cannot move... / But I must tell you... I chased the Urchins into the cemetery's underground crypt... / But the crypt has been violated by yet another bizarre evil -- hordes of Undead! I saw them near the entrance, commanded by a woman's voice. / She was chanting...  `War03c:KalenSpeech`

## An official or a mentor explains

- Wild beasts have invaded the Mana Mines, threatening many workers. One from our ranks must go, take charge and tame these abominable creatures.  `Con02a:AldwinImp`
- Hello, I am Aldwyn, the Master Conjurer for the Village of Ix. / What might a Warrior be doing so far afield from his homeland? Hmm, let me guess... the Mayor has asked you to help him with some domestic problem. / Well, I don't really care for Theogrin and his petty conundrums, but allow me to assist you, Warrior. / Take these beast scrolls. They might help during your adventures.  `War03b:AldwynIntro`
- Five workers are trapped in the mines below us. If we hurry we may be able to save all of them. Follow me! / I can guard and operate the elevators while you search for the workers. Take this key and use it to release the first worker. He will have another key to give you when you free him.  `Con03B.scr:Worker1B`
- I have heard the Heart of Nox has a complex and dangerous security system.  `Wiz07.scr:WardenTalk01`
- I'm today's contest judge. Today's best score is 8 out of 10 hits on the target. You need at least 9 to win. / Each entrant gets a quiver with 20 arrows. This one is yours. Take it to the firing line when you're ready.  `Con02a:ContestGreeting`

## A deal, a pitch, a bargain

- Hail, cell-brother! Morgan Lightfingers' the name! You seem like a man on a mission, so I thought I might offer this help. The last time I was arrested, they threw me in the very cell you're in now... And I escaped by digging a tunnel. The entrance should still be hidden under the cot in your cell. I'll keep an eye out for the warden while you search for it.  `War07A.scr:MorganTalk02`
- Hello again, do you wish to purchase another of my fine creatures? I think my purse will hold another 200 gold pieces. Heh, heh, heh...  `War08b:HenrickSalesPitchB`
- Greetings, young Adept. You're a long ways from Galava. I've taken great pains and personal expense, to bring this fine merchandise to these stricken people.  `War05A.scr:GypsyGreeting`
- Ahhhh! Welcome to Ix, stranger! If you're here for the Mayor's archery contest, go on in!  `Con02a:Gatekeeper2Greet`

## Gossip that turns into a pointer

- Aldwyn the Conjurer? Yeah, I know of him. Something of a hermit. / Go on the path, east out of town, across the bridge. You'll find it.  `Con02a:BarkeeperQuest`
- You know, too many disheartening reports have passed through here in the last few days, with no good news! The Tombs of Valor have been desecrated -- cadavers summoned by the Dark Necromancer Vows! I heard too that the old warlord Horrendous met his bloody fate on the blade of an Undead's sword!  `War08a:BarkeeperGreet`
- Hubris, pride, arrogance, call it what you will, the cause of my downfall. But dwelling on my fate is now moot. You must recover my Halberd before the Necromancer leaves Dün Mir, or all is lost. / Use the halberd, complete the incorporation with the Staff of Oblivion and defeat Hecubah!... I fear I am done, lad.  `War06a:HorrendousPride`
