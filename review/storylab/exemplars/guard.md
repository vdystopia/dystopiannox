# Guards, gatekeepers, wardens

Westwood's own lines (Nox, 1999), from the campaign's string table. Imitate their rhythm, register, corniness and how people are named; never copy a line or reuse a Westwood name. Rules, warnings, directions, pride in the place, curt orders. Short imperatives.

A page break inside a speech shows as ` / `. The key says who speaks and where (Con = Conjurer, War = Warrior, Wiz = Wizard campaign; the number is the chapter).
