Scope (review/qa2/ledger.md: a set note, or two or more Fix verdicts or notes): tavern (set note), throne room (set

**One root cause under three of the notes (Q3C-5).** `mapgen/nox.py` decides which floor seams lie at a wall, where no
edge piece is drawn (TW-12). It read a floor tile as centred at grid corner (x + 1, y + 1); the editor draws it at
(x + 1/2, y + 3/2) (MapEditor `render/TileRenderer.cs`, the same half-tile offset as Q2T-2's carpets). Read so, a NE or
SW (\) wall seemed to run through the middle of the tiles on its line, and the seam between the row of tiles along a NE
wall and the row inside it, two units in from the wall and in plain view, drew no edge. Every brick band, carpet trim
and hearth patch lying on that row lost its border there: RM-GREATHALL-01's carpet ("The northeast side of the carpet
does not have a proper border"), every great hall's band along its NE wall, RM-KITCHEN-05's hearth. The seams are now
read where the editor draws the tiles (`nox.TILE_CENTRE`); the checker's `floors.wall_blend` reads the same seams.
